## Financial Intelligence Platform for Industrial Operations

> Demonstrative project developed by LTX Services Co. Brazil for financial intelligence, business analytics and data-driven decision support.

---

## About the Project

This project presents a demonstrative Financial Intelligence solution designed for industrial operations.

The solution integrates financial, operational and management information to support decision-making in areas such as:

- Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)
- Controllership
- Cash Flow Management
- Budgeting & Forecasting
- OPEX and CAPEX Management
- Cost Analysis
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Business Intelligence
- Data Analytics
- Artificial Intelligence

All data used in this demonstration are simulated and do not represent confidential information from any company.

---

## Business Challenge

Industrial companies frequently operate with information distributed across ERP systems, spreadsheets, databases and operational applications.

This scenario can result in:

- fragmented information;
- manual consolidation;
- limited visibility of financial performance;
- delayed management reporting;
- difficulties in forecasting;
- inconsistent indicators;
- limited integration between financial and operational data.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how these challenges can be addressed through data integration, analytics, automation and intelligent financial management.
